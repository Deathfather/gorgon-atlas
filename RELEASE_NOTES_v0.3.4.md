# Gorgon Atlas v0.3.4

Release date: 2026-08-06

## What changed

- Rebuilt the area dropdown from the complete public catalog instead of relying mainly on creature-area categories.
- Added peaceful and NPC-only locations, including New Prestonbule Cave and its 8 published NPC positions.
- Expanded the selector from 59 to 81 entries: 15 world zones and hubs, 63 dungeons or special areas, and 3 private or utility areas.
- Made all 311 positioned NPC records and all 279 unique official landmark/travel markers reachable in the app.
- Reconciled equivalent Wiki and official-feed names so the same place is not shown twice under avoidable aliases.
- Kept broad official cave-system markers in their honest parent entry when the public data does not identify a specific child dungeon.
- Added a release audit that rejects missing public areas, duplicate dropdown names, or markers attached to nonexistent areas.
- Refreshed the cached public data from Elder Game v481 and the Project: Gorgon Wiki on 2026-08-06.

Enemy coordinates are still not published by the official feed. The 1,347 enemy and boss entries continue to show the Wiki's written location as authoritative and label their visual pins as approximate or area-wide.

## Verified

- 81 selectable areas
- 1,347 enemy-location records, including 241 boss records
- 311 positioned NPCs
- 279 unique official landmarks and travel markers
- 13 attributed map images
- 0 orphan markers
- Source-free Windows x64 package startup
- Eltibule duplicate-key regression
- New Prestonbule Cave selection and NPC display

## Install

Download `GorgonAtlas-v0.3.4.zip`, extract the whole folder, and run `GorgonAtlas.exe`. Keep the bundled `Data` and `Assets` folders beside the executable.
